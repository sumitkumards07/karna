Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect information necessary to facilitate your stay at Karnataka Pravasi Soudha, Tirumala. This includes your name, contact details, and identification documents (like Aadhaar) as required by state and temple administration regulations.

2. Use of Information

Your personal information is used exclusively for booking management, identity verification during check-in, and complying with security guidelines set by the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) and local authorities.

3. Data Protection

We are committed to securing your personal information. We do not share, sell, or distribute your personal data to third-party commercial entities.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding how your data is handled, please contact our front desk directly at +91 99999 99999.
