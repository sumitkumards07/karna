Pilgrimage to Tirumala is an inward spiritual journey of profound devotion. After hours of climbing the holy footpaths or navigating Darshan queues, what every devotee needs is clean, dependable, and peaceful shelter.

Karnataka Pravasi Soudha was established to provide devotees with an unhurried, hygienic sanctuary away from commercial clamor. We focus on what truly matters to visiting families: crisp fresh linens, hot water for early morning sacred baths, ground floor rooms for venerable grandparents, and an environment rooted in South Indian hospitality.

Carry original physical Government Photo IDs (Aadhaar is primary) matching the names on your Darshan and Soudha booking confirmations. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and large electronic cameras must be deposited at TTD cloakrooms before entry.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed clarifications about accommodation bookings, check-in timings, and pilgrimage operational rules.

1. How can I book a room in advance at Karnataka Pravasi Soudha? expand_more
You can enquire and reserve directly through our official reception helpline (+91 94808 87000) or via the dedicated WhatsApp Booking Desk. Provide your dates, pilgrim headcount, and preferred room type (Standard Non-AC, AC, or Family 4-Bed). The reception team will verify real-time availability and confirm booking details directly.
2. What are the standard check-in and check-out timings? expand_more
Our standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (Noon), and check-out time is 11:00 AM. A 24-hour cycle may be facilitated based on availability for devotees with midnight or early-hour Darshan arrival times upon advance communication with the front desk.
3. Is Karnataka Pravasi Soudha the official TTD accommodation booking office? expand_more
No. We are an independent pilgrim guest house providing accommodation support for devotees visiting Tirumala. Official TTD cottages (like Padmavathi, Varahaswamy, Shankhumitta) and Darshan passes must be secured exclusively through TTD's official portal (tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.in). Pravasi Soudha provides reliable non-TTD guest rooms.
4. Are hot water and AC facilities available in all rooms? expand_more
Air conditioning is available in our Standard AC and Family 4-Bed suites. Hot water is supplied across all categories from 4:00 AM to 9:00 AM daily via solar and centralized geyser systems, ensuring devotees can take their mandatory preparatory bath before morning prayers.
5. Can senior citizens request lower-floor or ground-floor rooms? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. We prioritize ground-floor rooms and rooms with Western commodes for senior citizens and devotees with physical mobility constraints. Please mention this requirement during your WhatsApp or phone booking inquiry.
6. What identification documents are mandatory at check-in? expand_more
Under Andhra Pradesh Police and Tirumala security regulations, physical Government Photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) is strictly mandatory for every adult guest during physical check-in. Digital photos on phones alone may not be accepted without verification.
