Sanctuary of Rest

A Practical, Dignified Place to Stay in Tirumala

Pilgrimage to Tirumala is an inward spiritual journey of profound devotion. After hours of climbing the holy footpaths or navigating Darshan queues, what every devotee needs is clean, dependable, and peaceful shelter.

Karnataka Pravasi Soudha was established to provide devotees with an unhurried, hygienic sanctuary away from commercial clamor. We focus on what truly matters to visiting families: crisp fresh linens, hot water for early morning sacred baths, ground floor rooms for venerable grandparents, and an environment rooted in South Indian hospitality.
